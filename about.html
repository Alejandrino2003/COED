About the department

Training teachers for basic, junior, and senior high school.

Who we are

The College of Education trains students to teach in basic, junior, and senior high school. Our students learn how to plan lessons, manage a classroom, and assess learning, then practice these skills in real schools during their teaching internship.

The BSEd program prepares future teachers with strong subject knowledge, classroom skills, and a heart for service.

Become the teacher your students remember.

What you will learn

Plan
Design lessons that fit your subject and your learners.
Manage
Build a classroom where every student can take part.
Assess
Measure learning fairly and use results to improve.
Practice
Teach in real schools during your internship.
