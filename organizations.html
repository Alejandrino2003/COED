Student organizations

Lead, serve, and grow beyond the classroom.

Get involved

Student organizations are where future teachers practice leading, planning, and serving before they ever step into a classroom. Whether you want a voice in college decisions or a hand in telling the college’s story, there is a team for you.

COEDSO

The student government. 6 executive officers, 3 class presidents, and 8 committee heads run projects for every student in the college.

COED Media

The multimedia team. 17 members cover graphic design, photography, cinematography, and video editing for college events.

Why join?

  • Build leadership skills you will use as a teacher, from managing a team to running an event
  • Earn real experience in planning, documentation, public speaking, and community outreach
  • Meet fellow future teachers across all year levels
  • Be part of our college and fulfill your role in shaping its culture and community

Want to join?

Ask the department which organizations are open to you, and when the next call for officers or members is.
